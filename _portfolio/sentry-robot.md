The sentry robot is the **only fully-automated robot** in the RoboMaster competition, required to operate in high-intensity, complex adversarial environments.

This project integrates:

* **SLAM-based localization and mapping** for autonomous navigation
* **Behavior Tree (BT)** architecture for real-time decision-making
* **Autonomous navigation** under dynamic, adversarial conditions
